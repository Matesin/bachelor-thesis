\chap[conclusion] Conclusion

In this thesis, we managed to implement the NFC functionality onto the original LionKey stack. The solution works according to the FIDO specification on WebAuthn-enabled websites, as shown in the \ref[evaluation] Chapter. 

\nl Although the implementation of NFC was successful and, therefore, the goal of this thesis was achieved, we must note that the prospects of the future development of the project are grim. As stated in the \ref[implementation] Chapter, STMicroelectronics currently does not offer commercial chips that would allow both energy harvesting and CTAP communication. In fact, a full self-standing solution is possible with an alternative chip in an SE configuration (preferably a commercially available one from NXP product line). However, in order to achieve a complete alternative to YubiKey, a complete redesign of the system's architecture would be necessary.
