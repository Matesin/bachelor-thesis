\chap[nfc-theory] NFC

NFC (Near Field Communication) is a short-range wireless communication technology. It enables point-to-point communication between devices in close proximity, the maximum distance being 7-10 centimeters and the optimal about 4 centimeters \cite[rfid-nfc-introduction]. The technology operates at a carrier frequency of 13.56 MHz and is based on inductive coupling between antennas, utilizing electromagnetic field. It is  standardized primarily by the ISO/IEC and the NFC Forum.
